Privacy Policy for ZAYTA Robotics and Technologies

We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users, customers, and website visitors ("Data Principals"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, the specific purposes for which it is processed, how you can exercise your statutory rights, and how you can register a grievance.

1. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the personal data that is strictly necessary for the purposes specified below:

  • Identity and Contact Data: Full name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing addresses, and account credentials when you create an account, place an order, or submit an inquiry.
  • Financial and Transaction Data: Order history, GST details (if applicable), and billing records. Payment card or UPI details are processed directly by authorized third-party payment gateways and are not stored on ZAYTA servers.
  • Robotics Telemetry and Device Data: Hardware serial numbers, firmware versions, battery health, diagnostic error logs, and network connectivity data generated by ZAYTA robotics hardware and companion applications.
  • Sensor and Environmental Data: Depending on the robotics product, onboard sensors (such as cameras, LiDAR, or microphones) may capture spatial mapping or voice commands to execute core device functions. Unless you explicitly opt in to cloud-assisted features, this data is processed locally on the device and is not transmitted to ZAYTA.
  • Digital Usage Data: IP address, device identifiers, browser type, and interaction logs collected via cookies when you access our website or applications.

2. Purpose of Processing and Lawful Basis

Under the DPDP Act, we process your personal data either on the basis of your free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous consent, or for certain legitimate uses permitted under Section 7 of the Act.

  • Contractual Fulfillment & Service Delivery: To process orders, deliver robotics hardware, manage user accounts, provide warranty support, and push essential firmware or security updates.
  • Product Diagnostics and Calibration: To troubleshoot hardware or software malfunctions, calibrate sensors, and maintain operational safety.
  • Communication & Alerts: To send order confirmations, critical safety notices, and policy updates.
  • Marketing (Consent-Based): To send newsletters, product launches, or promotional offers, strictly when you have opted in to receive them.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable Indian laws, tax regulations, court orders, or lawful requests from government authorities.

3. Consent and Withdrawal of Consent

  • Obtaining Consent: Where consent is the basis for processing, we will present a clear notice detailing the personal data to be collected and the exact purpose of processing before collecting your data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time with the same ease with which you gave it. You can withdraw consent by using the account settings in our app/website, clicking the "unsubscribe" link in emails, or contacting our Grievance Officer.
  • Effect of Withdrawal: Withdrawing consent will not affect the legality of any processing carried out before withdrawal. However, if you withdraw consent for data essential to operating connected robotics features, certain cloud-dependent functions may become unavailable.

4. Data Sharing and Cross-Border Transfers

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share personal data only with:

  • Data Processors: Trusted third-party service providers (such as cloud hosting providers, logistics partners, payment gateways, and customer support platforms) bound by valid contracts requiring them to maintain strict security safeguards.
  • Legal and Regulatory Authorities: Government agencies or law enforcement bodies when mandated under Indian law.
  • Cross-Border Data Transfers: Your personal data may be stored or processed on secure servers located in India or in other jurisdictions permitted by the Central Government of India under Section 16 of the DPDP Act. We ensure all cross-border transfers comply with applicable government notifications and security standards.

5. Data Retention and Erasure

In accordance with Section 8(7) of the DPDP Act, we retain your personal data only until:

  1. You withdraw your consent; or
  2. It is reasonable to assume that the specified purpose for which the data was collected is no longer being served, whichever is earlier—unless longer retention is required under any applicable Indian law (such as tax or accounting statutes).

Once the purpose is fulfilled and statutory retention periods expire, we permanently erase your personal data and instruct our Data Processors to do the same.

6. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under Sections 11 to 14 of the DPDP Act, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access Information: Request a summary of your personal data being processed by ZAYTA, the processing activities undertaken, and the identities of all Data Processors and third parties with whom your data has been shared.
  • Right to Correction and Erasure: Request the correction of inaccurate or misleading personal data, completion of incomplete data, updating of outdated data, and erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Have any complaint regarding our data processing practices or the exercise of your rights resolved within the timelines prescribed under the DPDP Rules.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate any other individual who shall, in the event of your death or incapacity, exercise your rights as a Data Principal on your behalf.

To exercise any of these rights, please write to our Grievance Officer using the contact details in Section 9.

7. Children's Personal Data

ZAYTA's websites, software, and robotics products are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. In compliance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act, we do not knowingly collect or process personal data of children without obtaining verifiable parental or lawful guardian consent. We do not engage in tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data without verifiable parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can erase the data.

8. Security Safeguards

As a Data Fiduciary, ZAYTA implements reasonable technical and organizational security safeguards—including end-to-end encryption, role-based access controls, network firewalls, and regular vulnerability assessments—to prevent personal data breaches. In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and each affected Data Principal in the manner and timeframe prescribed by law.
