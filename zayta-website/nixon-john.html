About Nixon John

Purpose-led leadership for practical innovation.

Nixon John is the Founder, Head and Chief Executive Officer of ZAYTA Robotics and Technologies LLP, based in Kottarakara, Kollam, Kerala.

As the visionary behind ZAYTA, he leads the company's mission and strategic direction, guiding the work of nine specialized divisions across robotics, artificial intelligence, automation, education, digital services, business solutions, agriculture, smart home technology and emerging technologies.

Nixon's leadership is rooted in solving real problems. At ZAYTA, technology begins with a clear purpose rather than a passing trend. Hardware, embedded systems, software and hands-on learning are brought together to create practical solutions that individuals, institutions and businesses can use in everyday life.

The principles guiding his leadership.

A practical approach that keeps ZAYTA focused on useful, lasting technology.

Think differently Start with the problem, question conventional limits and find smarter pathways instead of following trends.
Build practically Turn ideas into real solutions through rapid prototyping, rigorous testing and dependable deployment.
Improve continuously Refine and adapt ZAYTA's work as technology, opportunities and client needs evolve.
