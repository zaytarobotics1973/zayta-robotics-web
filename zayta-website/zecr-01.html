ZAYTA Emotional Companion Robot

A New Era of Human–Robot Companionship.

ZAYTA Robotics and Technologies proudly introduces ZECR-01 (ZAYTA Emotional Companion Robot) — an upcoming AI-powered humanoid companion designed to bring intelligent interaction, friendly communication and emotional companionship closer to everyday life.

ZECR-01 is being developed with a vision of creating a more meaningful connection between humans and technology.

Introducing a New Kind of Companion

Our upcoming robotic platform is being designed around several intelligent capabilities:

  • Emotion-Aware Interaction: Designed to recognize facial expressions and conversational cues.
  • Intelligent Communication: AI-powered natural conversations.
  • Multilingual Interaction: Planned support for Malayalam and English.
  • Expressive Personality: Human-friendly interaction with expressive visual responses.
  • Companion Assistance: Designed to support companionship and everyday interaction.
  • Human-Centred Design: A compact humanoid form developed with accessibility and usability in mind.

Our Vision

We believe technology should do more than perform tasks. It should help people feel connected, supported and understood.

ZECR-01 represents our vision of bringing intelligent, emotionally aware robotics into homes, care environments and everyday life.
