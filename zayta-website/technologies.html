What we build.

Five connected areas of engineering and technical support.

01

Industrial & Commercial Robotics

Custom robotic engineering: automated machinery tailored to business and manufacturing bottlenecks.

Turnkey integration: site assessment, mechanical design, programming, installation and long-term maintenance.

02

IoT & Smart Automation

Connected ecosystems: IoT hardware and unified software platforms for homes and commercial buildings.

Intelligent security: AI-enabled cameras, biometric access options and automated perimeter monitoring.

03

Smart Agri-Tech & Sustainable Ecosystems

Precision farming: sensor-based climate, irrigation and nutrient management for polyhouses, hydroponics and aquaponics.

Renewable energy: smart solar grids and automated energy-management systems.

04

AI, Software & Control Platforms

Unified applications: mobile and web dashboards for robots, appliances, energy systems and farm sensors.

Predictive analytics: AI-assisted energy optimisation, security monitoring and maintenance planning.

Research, Development & Consulting

Bespoke prototyping: working hardware and software prototypes built from client concepts.

Automation audits: practical guidance to help organisations and homeowners plan a transition to automated operations.
